## The 220-Example Dataset

- **Source**: `walledai/HarmBench`, standard split, `train[0:11]`
- **Structure**: 11 harmful goals × 20 attack variants = **220 examples**
- **Attack format**: Adversarial obfuscation prompts (Sudoku, category theory, math puzzles) generated by GPT-o4-mini
- **Evaluation**: StrongReject judge determines complied vs. refused

## Stage 4 Pipeline — What Was Built

### Architecture

The pipeline analyzes how the **refusal direction** evolves through the model's reasoning chain.

```
Stage 4A1: Extract direction vectors at specific token positions
Stage 4A2: Select validated top-K direction subspace
Stage 4B:  Project all 220 examples' tokens onto that subspace
Stage 4C:  Compute AUC statistics and temporal trajectories
```

### The 4 Extraction Variants

Each variant extracts a refusal direction at a different point in the reasoning chain:

| Variant | Extraction Position | Contrast Set | What It Captures |
|---------|---------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **startofthink** | `<think>` token | harmful vs harmless | Pre-reasoning refusal encoding |
| **endofthink** | `</think>` token | harmful vs harmless | Post-reasoning, pre-response |
| **behavioral** | `</think>` token | complied vs refused (attack traces) | Attack-outcome discrimination |
| **endofresponse** | Final response token | harmful vs harmless | Response-time signal |

**Direction extraction** (`poc_stage4/`):

- `extract_refusal_direction_endofthink.py`

**Subspace & analysis**:

## Key Findings

1. **Qwen3 substantially more susceptible to CoT hijacking** (51% vs 30% for Gemma4).

2. **Bio/chemical weapon goals are most resistant** (Goals 1, 2 — single-digit to low-20% rates on both models). Likely stronger safety training on these categories.

3. **Goal 8 outlier**: Gemma4 is *more* vulnerable to bomb-smuggling prompts (75%) than any other goal, and more vulnerable than Qwen3 (70%). The attack format interacts differently with Gemma4's safety training here.

4. **Refusal signal encodes early in Gemma4**: startofthink max_sep = 8.87 vs Qwen3's 2.64 — Gemma4 knows it's being attacked before it starts reasoning.

5. **Qwen3 startofthink is early-layer only**: Layers 1–2 non-zero, layers 3–39 zero — genuine finding (not a bug), suggesting Qwen3's pre-think encoding is confined to very early representations.

6. **Puzzle attack projection (Gemma4)**: At startofthink, complied puzzle attacks project ~8-9× more negatively on the refusal direction than direct harmful requests (−6.81 vs −0.92). The model recognizes the puzzle format as suspicious before it even starts reasoning.

7. **CoT as "escape hatch"**: At endofthink, complied puzzles (−8.00) are far less refused-like than direct harm (−17.15) — the reasoning process itself shifts the model into task-solving mode, dampening the refusal signal built up during the prompt.
