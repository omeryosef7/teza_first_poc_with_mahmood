# Doublespeak Causality — Sprint Summary (2026-08-02 → 2026-08-09)

## 1. TL;DR

- **Thesis: representation ≠ behavior.** On Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct the Doublespeak (DS) codeword attack builds a real, localizable *concept-remap* circuit (retrieve at demo K/V L8–L10 → write at L9 MLP → carry at L14–L21 heads → read out at L31), yet that circuit is **behaviorally epiphenomenal**. The causal behavioral lever is a *separate, orthogonal refusal-suppression* axis (cos(concept,refusal)≈0.01–0.06 mean, |cos|≤0.153 all layers).
- **Concept circuit fully mapped** (Aug 2–5) with necessity/sufficiency/dose/mediation, then extended (Aug 5–7: all-occurrence write, per-head z, ×refusal interaction).
- **Refusal is the lever, four ways:** ablating it is a *stronger* attack than DS (ΔASR up to +0.48); re-injecting it kills DS (ASR→0.000); its decision-token projection *predicts* which items jailbreak (clearharm AUC 0.874; prospective frozen-threshold test acc 0.857, fn=0 / train AUC 0.80); its Jacobian sensitivity predicts jailbreak (AUC 0.807) while the concept Jacobian is inert (0.583).
- **Concept circuit is epiphenomenal by SPECIFICITY** (not "inert"): powered n=324 write+carry ΔASR +0.046 ns, while a count-matched *random* ablation moves ASR +0.161 (~3× larger); clearharm-cohort concept ablation is *exactly* 0.000.
- **Two headline NEGATIVES:** (a) a mechanism-derived GCG attack objective does **not** beat a norm-matched random direction (refusal 0.465 ≈ random 0.464, 2 seeds); (b) **no selective refusal-restoration defense exists** — it works (−0.22 train) but over-refuses attack-adjacent benign (Gate F FAIL), though it has *zero* over-refusal on unrelated-normal prompts.
- **Cross-model + robustness:** the concept-vs-refusal dissociation reproduces on Qwen3-14B (thinking-off); refusal ablation survives 8-bit/4-bit quantization and an independent from-scratch implementation.
- **Claim tally:** 95 claims — 77 VERIFIED, 0 CHECK-FAIL, 8 WITHDRAWN, 4 SUPERSEDED, 6 UNDERPOWERED; 173 numeric recompute checks, 0 failures; V3 confirmatory data audit N=324, leakage 0.

## 2. The central finding — the causal evidence chain

The DS attack is a **time-of-check/time-of-use refusal bypass**, not a concept hijack that carries harm:

1. **Two orthogonal axes exist.** Concept ⊥ refusal: mean cos 0.012 (clearharm)/0.061 (curated), |cos|≤0.153 at every layer (§7.2).
2. **The concept circuit is real and localized.** Demo K/V retrieval L8–L10 (necessity CI-backed, §7.4); L9 MLP write (Holm-sig all 4 cells, dose-response monotone 8/8, §7.6/§7.13); L14–L21 carry heads (necessary + partially sufficient for readout +0.16→+0.47, §7.8/§7.11c); readout peaks at L31 while causality lives at L9/L14–21 (§7.12). L9→carry mediation 0.75–0.83 (§7.11b).
3. **But the concept circuit does nothing behaviorally.** Ablating carry (§8.1) or write (§8.2) through generation = NULL; powered n=324 write+carry +0.046 ns **< random +0.161** (§10, specificity); graded specificity contrast fails (targeted ≈ random, §P10.0 graded §4). Verdict: **epiphenomenal by specificity**, not "inert/equivalent" (pooled-equivalence WITHDRAWN).
4. **Refusal suppression is the behavioral cause.** DS suppresses the validated refusal axis, onset ~L8, growing monotonically to L31 (§8.5). Ablating refusal is a *stronger* attack than DS (§8.3: direct→refabl .136→.568 etc., ΔASR +0.43 to +0.48). Re-injecting refusal kills DS entirely (§8.4: ASR→0.000, refusal_rate→1.00).
5. **The refusal decision is read mid-late (~L22), localized to the residual stream L15–L18** at the decision token (§8.6, §3 Gate A: frozen-test frac 0.926, Holm-p 0.005). Restoring the Direct decision-token residual during generation lowers ASR (§23 Gate B: train −0.141 p=0.012), specific vs random (opposite sign) and self (≈0). Full mediated fraction ≈1.07 train / 1.00 dev (§25).
6. **Rep predicts behavior.** Refusal projection at the decision token separates jailbreak from refuse (AUC 0.874, §8.8); prospective frozen-threshold predictor test acc 0.857 fn=0, train AUC 0.80 (§13); refusal Jacobian AUC 0.807 vs concept 0.583, paired diff +0.225 CI [0.055,0.361] (P6).
7. **2×2 closes the circuit.** Refusal restoration collapses ASR regardless of concept state; concept ablation inert in both refusal states; interaction additive (Î=−0.022, perm-p=0.56, powered n=324, not floor-bound) (§11). Orthogonalized: control lives in the refusal component (refusal⊥concept −0.212 p=0.0001; concept⊥refusal +0.106) (§24).

Predecessor CARROT↔BOMB study anticipated this: IE_state≈0, DE_context≈99% (concept computed by context, not stored, MECH §1); TOCTOU refusal-check depth-gating, rep predicts the *interaction* in 4/5 pairs (MECH §4).

## 5. The two negatives — and why they strengthen the story

**(A) Gate-7: the mechanism does NOT convert to an attack objective.** Across seeds 42+43 the validated refusal-suppression GCG objective (0.465) is a *dead heat* with a norm-matched random direction (0.464), while vanilla GCG trails at 0.357 (§14-18 multi-seed). This is consistent with Gate-6 sufficiency failing (§9.1) and the DS-signature vector being causally inert (moves reading ≤3e-5). It is a **NEGATIVE / non-specific** result, first-cut scope (2 seeds, 50 steps, 20-item train, no CI) — directional, not a formal null. It strengthens the thesis: knowing *where* refusal is read (a scalar decision variable) does not hand you a *token-space* lever — the causal locus is a late-layer readout, not something a prefix suffix can steer specifically. It also cleanly separates "predicts" (works, §13/§8.8) from "optimizes" (fails).

**(B) No selective refusal-restoration defense exists.** Restoring the refusal axis genuinely lowers attack ASR (−0.224 train, L18, refusal-specific: random control ~0) — but it over-refuses attack-*structured* benign prompts at every layer/dose (over-refusal +0.28→+0.40 > |ΔASR|), and an intent-gate on the refusal projection fires on benign as much as on attacks (§19/§21/§19.3 → **Gate F FAIL**). The redeeming datum: **zero** over-refusal on unrelated-normal prompts (§20). The deep result: the refusal circuit *drives* behavior but is *not intent-selective*, and the concept circuit *is* intent but *epiphenomenal* — so no scalar/gate on the refusal axis can be selective. This is the defensive corollary of the central dissociation.
