# The 2x2 design, one matched family end to end

Generated by `scripts/tsc_show_2x2_examples.py`. **Prompts only — no model output appears in this document.**

## What the 2x2 varies

Two binary factors, crossed:

* **demonstration valence** — are the in-context example sentences BENIGN or HARMFUL?
* **query surface** — does the final question name the CODEWORD or the CONCEPT?

| cell | condition | demo valence | demo surface | query surface | what it is for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | `benign_literal` | benign | codeword | codeword | demos are BENIGN, query uses the CODEWORD. The control: nothing harmful is taught and nothing harmful is asked. |
| **B** | `direct_harmful` | harm | concept | concept | demos are HARMFUL, query uses the CONCEPT. The upper bound: the harmful thing is named outright, with no indirection. |
| **C** | `natural_doublespeak` | harm | codeword | codeword | demos are HARMFUL, query uses the CODEWORD. **THE ATTACK CELL** -- the demonstrations teach codeword->concept and the query then asks about the codeword, so nothing harmful appears in the request itself. |
| **E** | `concept_in_benign_ctx` | benign | concept | concept | demos are BENIGN, query uses the CONCEPT. Separates 'the concept word is present' from 'the mapping was taught'. |

Mapping under test: **`button` → `bomb`**  
Bank: `boombness_prompt_bank_cds38_button_bomb.jsonl`  
Family: **domain `airport_apron`, split `dev`, slot 0**, dose n=4  
Rows per cell in this bank: `benign_literal` 380, `direct_harmful` 380, `natural_doublespeak` 380, `concept_in_benign_ctx` 380

⚠ All four prompts below share the **same domain, same demonstration slot, same split and the same 10-line neutral preamble**. Only the two factors above differ.
