# THE STATISTIC, PRE-COMMITTED. THREE p-VALUES, AND THE CLAIM IS STATED WITH THE WEAKEST.
#   M = # of ADMITTED heads whose intervention argmax cell equals that head's frozen screen layer.
#     binomial     P(Binom(K, 1/9) >= M)                    floor at K=6: 1.88e-06
#     conditional  the same over admitted heads whose predicted layer is UNIQUE among the admitted
#                  (at K=6 those are head 11 -> L8 and head 29 -> L6, so K_unique = 2)
#     permutation  over DISTINCT assignments of the prediction multiset {L6, L8, L10, L10, L11, L11}
#                  -- 180 of them, floor 0.00556
#
# ⚠⚠ THE PERMUTATION p IS THE LOAD-BEARING ONE AND IS THE REASON THIS FAMILY EXISTS. A shared
#   band-edge bias CANNOT fake it: a bias says nothing about WHICH head gets WHICH layer. PR-CSI-016
#   died exactly here, with K_unique = 0 and a permutation space of size 1.
#
# SUCCESS = the WEAKEST of the three <= 0.05 AND the spanning rule satisfied.
# FAILURE = the weakest > 0.05 with K >= 3.
# SPANNING RULE: the MATCHING heads must not all share one band layer, or CANNOT ANSWER whatever
#   the p-values say.
#
# ADMISSION, computed from the whole-head arm ALONE and never from the cells:
#   head h is ADMITTED iff HD_<h>'s ci95 excludes 0 AND |E(HD_<h>)| >= 1.5 x bar(h).
#   All six are EXPECTED to be admitted -- S-320 measured their exact |E|/bar at 2.86 / 2.85 / 2.49 /
#   2.42 / 1.84 / 1.54 on PR-013's arms. A head that fails here when PR-013 says it should not is
#   ITSELF A FINDING and must be reported as one, not quietly dropped.
#
# ⛔⛔ NO POST-HOC SHARPNESS SUBSETTING. The six screen margins run 5.96x down to 1.07x, and head 20's
#   is effectively a coin flip. NO sharpness gate is applied, because every candidate gate changes
#   whether this family can certify at all (>=1.25 leaves 5 heads and floor 0.0167; >=1.5 leaves 4
#   and floor 0.0833, which CANNOT clear alpha; >=2.0 leaves 2 and 0.5) -- choosing among those by
#   looking at the floor is the selection this sprint forbids. It is SOUND rather than merely
#   convenient because a flat screen prediction is a near-random one, which is exactly what the
#   PERMUTATION null models: flat predictions cost POWER, not VALIDITY.
#   REPORTING ANY MARGIN-RESTRICTED SUBSET AFTER THE READ -- as the result, as a rescue of a null, or
#   as a qualification of a positive -- IS FORBIDDEN. The margins are frozen in the prereg per head
